## Pricing

Solo and Pro include a 14-day card-upfront trial. Annual billing is two months free.

### Solo
- $8/mo or $80/yr
- 50 GB project storage
- Unlimited devices
- 30-day version history

### Pro
- $20/mo or $200/yr
- 250 GB project storage
- Unlimited devices
- 365-day version history
- Larger manifests/projects
- Advanced hydration/ignore rules

### Team
- $12–15/user/mo
- 100–150 GB/user pooled storage
- Unlimited devices
- Shared workspaces
- Roles and audit log

### Extra Storage
- $3/mo or $30/yr per additional 100 GB
