1. HIGH — The existing error-generation machinery is not safely invocable for a deliberate restart. The error transition is trapped inside the watcher callback ([daemon.ts](/home/via/Development/Personal/rbox-core/.claude/worktrees/pull-fast-path/src/cli/daemon/daemon.ts:812)), while `startLiveWatch()` is startup-only: reusing it would duplicate safety/deep timers, replace debouncer/registry state, and leave the old watcher unclosed ([daemon.ts](/home/via/Development/Personal/rbox-core/.claude/worktrees/pull-fast-path/src/cli/daemon/daemon.ts:784)). More importantly, the clean-scan predicate only binds `errorGen`, coverage, and watcher presence ([daemon.ts](/home/via/Development/Personal/rbox-core/.claude/worktrees/pull-fast-path/src/cli/daemon/daemon.ts:2483)); it does not prove that the replacement subscription was live before the scan began. A scan overlapping close/re-subscribe can therefore re-trust despite an uncovered gap. Specify a serialized async `rearmWatcher`: downgrade P1 immediately, close/recreate all watcher-associated resources exactly once, and permit re-trust only from a full-tree scan started after the new watcher session is installed. Deliberate re-arms must not count toward the transient-drop fuse, and need defined behavior when `RBOX_WATCHER_RETRUST=0`.

2. HIGH — `nativePruneGlobs(root) + gitRepos key set` is insufficient for Chokidar. Parcel’s subscription input is exactly `nativePruneGlobs` ([watcher.ts](/home/via/Development/Personal/rbox-core/.claude/worktrees/pull-fast-path/src/cli/daemon/watcher.ts:382)), but Chokidar’s recursive admission uses the complete `matcher.prunes` result ([watcher.ts](/home/via/Development/Personal/rbox-core/.claude/worktrees/pull-fast-path/src/cli/daemon/watcher.ts:423)). Removing an ordinary rule such as `foo/` changes Chokidar admission without changing `nativePruneGlobs`; the facade cannot add watches beneath the formerly pruned subtree. Require a backend-specific fingerprint—full effective admission inputs for Chokidar. Computing the Parcel fingerprint itself is deterministic and cheap: two synchronous rule-file reads plus a small fixed-list calculation ([ignore.ts](/home/via/Development/Personal/rbox-core/.claude/worktrees/pull-fast-path/src/engine/ignore.ts:227)).

3. The deep/safety boundary guard is otherwise correctly placed in concept, provided it runs before `errorGenAtStart`, audit creation, and `watcherScanMode()`. Downgrading P1 makes safety scans unpruned, while deep scans are already unconditionally unpruned, so pinned rescans and scan-mode selection do not conflict. The restart must be awaited before that scan begins to close finding 1.

Verdict: **CHANGES-REQUIRED**.
